AUDIO & VOICE DISCLOSURE

Use of Audio & Voice Tools
Cantrell Creatives provides audio and voice-generation tools to assist creators with narration, audiobooks, previews, and other spoken-word content.
Audio may be generated using artificial intelligence–based text-to-speech services, including third-party providers.

Role of the Creator
Creators are fully responsible for:
	• Reviewing all generated audio before use or publication
	• Confirming pronunciation, pacing, tone, and accuracy
	• Ensuring audio content aligns with their intended purpose
Generated audio should be treated as a working output, not a final product, until reviewed and approved by the creator.

Voice Selection & Variability
Audio output may vary based on:
	• Selected voice
	• Language and regional settings
	• Text structure and formatting
	• Third-party service behavior
Cantrell Creatives does not guarantee consistency across voices, sessions, or providers.

Temporary Storage & Retention
Unless otherwise stated by membership level:
	• Generated audio files are considered temporary
	• Audio may be deleted after applicable retention periods
	• Deleted audio files cannot be recovered
Creators are responsible for downloading and backing up any audio they wish to retain.

Third-Party Audio Services
Audio generation may utilize third-party services such as:
	• Microsoft Azure Text-to-Speech
	• Google Text-to-Speech
	• Other supported providers
Use of these services is subject to their respective terms, limitations, and availability.
Cantrell Creatives is not responsible for service interruptions, voice changes, or provider-level restrictions.

Ownership & Usage
Creators retain ownership of audio generated from their content.
However:
	• Ownership does not imply guaranteed storage
	• Audio files not downloaded within retention periods may be removed
	• Cantrell Creatives does not claim authorship or rights to creator audio

Important Notice
ATTENTION:
Creators should download and back up all audio files immediately after generation.
Cantrell Creatives is not liable for loss of audio due to technical issues, expired retention periods, or failure to download files.
