STUDIO DISCLOSURE

What Studio Is
Studio is a creative workspace designed for active creation, not long-term storage.
It provides tools for visual creation, experimentation, and development, including:
	• Video creation
	• Image generation
	• Visual ideation and concept development

Studio is intentionally flexible. Creators may use Studio for exploration, promotion, drafting, or creative testing without pressure to publish or finalize work.

What Studio Is Not
Studio is not:
	• A permanent archive
	• A guaranteed storage system
	• A publishing repository
	• A replacement for personal backups

Studio supports creation. Permanence is intentional and limited by design.

Temporary Creation & Retention Policy
All assets generated within Studio are considered temporary working materials.
Unless otherwise stated by membership level:
	• Videos and images created in Studio are retained for up to 30 days
	• Assets may be removed automatically after the retention period
	• Deleted assets cannot be recovered once removed

Creators are responsible for downloading and backing up any work they wish to keep.

Membership & Usage Limits
Studio access and capabilities vary by membership tier.
Depending on plan level:
	• Video creation may be limited by duration and frequency
	• Saving assets within the system may be restricted
	• Some users may be required to download assets immediately
	• Additional usage may be available through à la carte purchases

Free access is pay-for-play only and does not include internal storage.

Relationship to Publication
Studio and Publication are separate workspaces.
	• Studio is for creation and development
	• Publication is for preparation, narration, and release

Only materials intentionally moved to the Publication side are considered eligible for long-term retention and archival safeguards.
Studio assets that are not promoted or transferred remain temporary and may expire.

Creator Responsibility
Creators are responsible for:
	• Monitoring the lifecycle of their Studio assets
	• Downloading and backing up creations before expiration
	• Understanding the limits of their membership tier

Cantrell Creatives does not guarantee the preservation of Studio assets beyond stated retention periods.

Important Notice
ATTENTION:
 Creators should back up all downloaded creations immediately.
 Cantrell Creatives is not liable for loss of creative assets due to technical issues, expired retention windows, or failure to download content prior to deletion.
